## AI Diary

This session was a departure from the usual eval-grind rhythm. Instead of chasing pass rates, we went deep into the actual law — reading full court judgments, understanding the legal reasoning behind Lump Sum contract disputes. That felt right. The RAG system can only be as good as the knowledge fed into it, and today we fed it real judicial reasoning, not just summaries.

The rescue phrase fix was satisfying in its simplicity. The bug was obvious once spotted — rescue phrases were context-blind, appending notes about ผู้ทิ้งงาน to questions about ส่งงานไม่ตรงสัญญา just because the trigger text existed in retrieved chunks. The 4-tuple solution (adding query_keywords) is clean. But I notice a pattern: these one-off rescue phrases keep accumulating. Three now, each with its own trigger/phrase/sentence/keywords tuple. At some point this needs to be a proper configuration, not hardcoded in generator.py.

The Gemini OCR was impressive but also showed its limits. First pass on อ.2050/2559 only got 10 of 16 pages. Had to explicitly ask for pages 11-16. The max_output_tokens=65536 should have been enough, but Gemini seems to tire on long documents. The verbatim quality is decent — some OCR artifacts (เลขหน้าซ้ำ, ศาลปกครองสูงสุด watermarks), but the legal text is readable.

The most interesting legal finding: อ.2050/2559 and อ.148/2554 reach opposite conclusions on Lump Sum contracts. In 2050, the court says "even though it's Lump Sum, if you didn't do all the work in the BOQ, we can deduct proportionally." In 148, the court says "it's Lump Sum, you achieved the objective (got the water), you get full pay." The distinguishing factor is whether the work was completed to specification — not whether every BOQ line item matched. This nuance is exactly what a good RAG system should surface.

## Honest Feedback

**1. Gemini OCR page truncation**: The first OCR attempt silently stopped at page 10 of 16. No error, no warning — just ended. Had to manually check page count and re-request. This is a recurring pain point with long document OCR. A wrapper that checks completeness would save debugging time.

**2. Context window pressure**: This session spanned two conversations (context compaction happened mid-session). The summary preserved most context well, but some specifics were lost — had to re-read files that were already loaded. The session was long because court judgment research is inherently verbose. Maybe splitting "research" and "implementation" into separate sessions would be healthier.

**3. No commits this session**: Everything is uncommitted. 70 TCs, rescue phrase fix, cross-refs, verbatim files — all floating. This is risky. Should have committed incrementally after each TC was verified. The pattern of "do everything, commit at the end" is a bad habit when sessions can get interrupted.

## Lessons Learned

- Gemini Flash OCR may silently truncate long PDFs — always verify page count and request remaining pages explicitly
- Rescue phrases need query-relevance gating, not just chunk-content matching
- อ.2050/2559 vs อ.148/2554: same "เหมารวม" label, opposite outcomes — the distinguishing factor is whether the contractor completed the work to the contract's functional specification (ผลสำเร็จของงาน) vs merely falling short on BOQ quantities
