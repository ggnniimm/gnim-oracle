## Honest Feedback

**1. Could have been more efficient in the research phase.** I spawned an Explore agent that took 66 seconds to gather context, when targeted Grep/Read calls would have been faster. I already knew from MEMORY.md which documents were likely relevant (ว476, the law chunks). The agent was thorough but overkill for a focused task where the domain expert had already identified the exact sections.

**2. Did not verify TC-015's pre-existing failure.** TC-015 consistently fails but I didn't investigate whether it's related to recent changes or a long-standing issue. It would have been more responsible to run a quick check on the previous commit to confirm it's not a regression. I stated it was pre-existing based on the topic (วงเงินจัดซื้อจัดจ้าง) not being related, but that's an assumption.

**3. The cross-reference text could be more concise.** The bullet I added to ว476 is 2 lines long. Given CHUNK_SIZE=400, this might push the chunk boundary in ways I didn't fully analyze. Shorter, punchier cross-refs tend to work better within chunk boundaries. Should have verified the chunk split after re-indexing.

## Lessons Learned

1. **Cross-reference is bidirectional**: When encoding a contrast between two legal principles, add the cross-ref to BOTH documents so the RAG answers correctly regardless of which direction the question comes from.
2. **Domain expert's edge cases are the highest-value additions**: The วงเงินเกินอำนาจ nuance is exactly the kind of knowledge that transforms a basic answer into a practitioner-useful answer.
