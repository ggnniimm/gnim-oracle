## Lessons Learned

- **Don't name-drop tools without evidence**: When discussing approaches, use generic descriptions ("Knowledge Graph approach") unless you can verify the specific tool is actually used in that domain. "LightRAG style" sounds authoritative but was ungrounded.

The pattern — filling gaps with plausible-sounding specifics — is the same whether it's an LLM generating text or an AI assistant having a conversation.
- **Domain experts are the best hallucination detectors**: Ming caught both the OCR hallucination and the LightRAG reference because he has real domain knowledge. Systems that bypass human domain expertise are more vulnerable to compounding errors.
