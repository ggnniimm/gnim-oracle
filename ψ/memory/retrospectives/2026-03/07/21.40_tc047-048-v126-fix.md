## AI Diary

This session taught me something important about intellectual humility in legal interpretation. When Ming asked me to discuss who "ผู้มีอำนาจ" means under Section 103, I confidently presented a table showing ว126 as evidence that it means "หัวหน้าหน่วยงานของรัฐ". I even put it in a neat markdown table as if it were settled law.

Ming caught it immediately -- ว126 doesn't actually establish that in its ข้อวินิจฉัย. The parenthetical "(หัวหน้าหน่วยงานของรัฐ)" appears only in the ข้อเท็จจริง section, which is background narrative, not the actual ruling. The ข้อวินิจฉัย itself only says "หน่วยงานของรัฐสามารถใช้ดุลพินิจ..." without specifying who within the agency.

This is exactly the kind of mistake that makes legal AI dangerous -- confidently citing a source but misrepresenting what part of the document actually says what. The OCR summary sections (บทสรุปสำหรับสืบค้น) had inherited this over-interpretation too, which means the RAG would propagate the error. Fixing the source document was the right call.

The broader lesson: in Thai legal documents, ข้อเท็จจริง vs ข้อวินิจฉัย carry very different weight. I need to be careful which section I'm drawing conclusions from.

## Honest Feedback

1. **Over-confident legal interpretation**: I presented the ว126 interpretation as if it were clear-cut, building a comparison table that made my (wrong) reading look authoritative. In legal work, I should flag uncertainty more explicitly, especially when the source text is ambiguous. The distinction between ข้อเท็จจริง and ข้อวินิจฉัย is fundamental in Thai legal documents -- I should have caught this myself.

2. **OCR summaries propagate bias**: The บทสรุปสำหรับสืบค้น sections in the markdown files are AI-generated during OCR. They can bake in interpretive errors that then get indexed and retrieved as if they were authoritative. This is a systemic risk -- one bad summary pollutes the entire RAG pipeline. We should consider whether these summary sections need review.

3. **CLI flag discovery**: Wasted a call trying `--filter` before finding `--id` was the correct flag for run_eval.py. Minor friction but avoidable if I had read the argparse first.

## Lessons Learned

- ข้อเท็จจริง vs ข้อวินิจฉัย carry different legal weight; don't cite background narrative as ruling
- AI-generated OCR summaries (บทสรุปสำหรับสืบค้น) can propagate interpretive errors into RAG
