## Honest Feedback

1. **Import chain fragility**: Testing retrieval was painful — I couldn't easily query the index because of lightrag dependency, IndexManager import chain, and no simple CLI query tool. Had to resort to creating a temp TC via the eval pipeline. A `python3 pipeline/query.py "search term"` that just works would save significant time. This friction means we rarely verify retrieval after indexing, which could hide problems.

2. **Flaky TCs accumulating**: TC-010, TC-011, and now TC-035 are all flaky from LLM variance. At 61 TCs, having 3 flaky ones means any full eval run has a ~15% chance of showing a false failure. We should either tighten the must_contain to be more reliably present, or mark them as known-flaky so they don't cause alarm.

3. **Legal analysis overconfidence**: I produced a confident but wrong analysis about ม.97 + ม.102 needing to be used together. The diagrams and tables made it look authoritative when it was actually flawed. I should be more careful to present legal interpretations as "one possible reading" rather than definitive statements, especially in a domain where I'm synthesizing from documents rather than having deep expertise.

## Lessons Learned

- Contract amendment (ม.97) that sets a new future deadline absorbs all prior delay — no penalty accrues until the NEW deadline passes
- ม.97 and ม.102 serve different functions: scope vs timing — they overlap only when the contractor exceeds even the amended deadline
- Appending to FAISS index works without rebuild — dedup.db prevents duplicates
