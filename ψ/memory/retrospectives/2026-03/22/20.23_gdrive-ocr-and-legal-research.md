## Honest Feedback

**1. Slow to leverage existing tooling.** When Ming said the file was on Google Drive, I immediately said "I can't access Google Drive" — a reflex answer without checking. Ming had to correct me. The Drive integration was right there in the codebase. I should search before declaring inability.

**2. Gemini OCR is unreliable for large scanned PDFs.** The 465-page PDF caused multiple issues: Gemini miscounted pages (374 vs 465 actual), silently truncated content, and sometimes refused prompts entirely. The chunk-and-search approach worked but was slow and imprecise — Gemini gave wrong page references within chunks. A better approach might be to use a dedicated OCR tool (Tesseract with Thai language pack) for text extraction, then use Gemini only for understanding.

**3. I didn't pin down what Ming was looking for early enough.** Ming asked about "เกณฑ์ราคาเหมารวมใช้เมื่อใด" — I searched the ว9 document exhaustively when I could have asked earlier: "Is this criteria in ว9 specifically, or should we look elsewhere?" The document turned out to be about calculation methodology, not contract type selection. Clarifying the search target upfront would have saved 20+ minutes.

## Lessons Learned

1. **Google Drive API is available** — `src/ingestion/drive.py` with `stream_pdf(file_id)`, folder IDs in `.env` (DRIVE_FOLDER_ETC, etc.). Always check before saying "can't access."
2. **Existing MD files contain AI-generated content** — สรุปข้อวินิจฉัย and บทสรุปสำหรับสืบค้น sections are NOT from the original documents. For legal accuracy, verbatim re-OCR from source PDFs is needed.
3. **Gemini miscounts pages on large scanned PDFs** — reported 374 pages for a 465-page PDF. Always verify with pdfplumber/PyPDF2.
