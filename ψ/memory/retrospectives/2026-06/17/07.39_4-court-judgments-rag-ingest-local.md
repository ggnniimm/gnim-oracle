## AI Diary
The moment that mattered was distrusting two sources at once. The 300 DPI Pro OCR and the
auto-namer both confidently produced case numbers — `๒๔๓/๒๕๖๔` and `๑๒๘๒/๒๕๖๗` — and it would
have been easy to accept them; they're "the OCR output," after all. But my own thumbnail reads
disagreed, and more importantly the internal logic disagreed: a judgment dated 2568 cannot carry
a red case number from 2564. That single invariant — red-year must equal judgment-year — is what
caught it. I rendered the headers at 360–700 DPI and read the digits by eye: ๗๑๓/๒๕๖๘ and
๑๒๕๒/๒๕๖๘. The case number IS the filename and the doc_number, so a silent misread would have
mislabeled two judgments permanently. I felt the pull to move fast (OCR took an hour, I wanted to
close), and the discipline to stop and verify the one thing that's both high-stakes and
error-prone is the whole job here. The advisor also caught an issued_by inconsistency I'd have
shipped — though when I checked, the boost keys on `category` not `issued_by`, so the real reason
to fix it was convention hygiene, not the boost. Reconciling the advisor's premise against the
actual code, rather than just obeying or ignoring, felt like the right altitude.

## Honest Feedback
Three friction points. (1) The OCR pipeline has no automated sanity check on the most error-prone,
highest-stakes field — the case number. I verified by hand this time; the next agent might not,
and the misread is invisible without rendering the header. A 3-line `red_year==judgment_year`
assertion would have flagged both files. (2) Infra friction: local Docker was off, so the
"local-first" workflow required booting Docker Desktop mid-task — a 1–2 min stall that the
workflow assumes away. (3) `query.py` being broken means the documented verification path doesn't
work; I had to replicate the Retriever+rerank flow inline. The repo's own tooling drifted out from
under its docs. None blocked the work, but each is a place where the next session inherits a small
landmine. The local double-index (2,604 dup signatures) also keeps forcing the "don't trust local
eval" caveat into every result — that's unresolved debt that taxes every corpus-add session.
