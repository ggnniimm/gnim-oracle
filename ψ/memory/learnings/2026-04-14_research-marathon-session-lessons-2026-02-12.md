# ## Research Marathon Session Lessons (2026-02-12)

## Research Marathon Session Lessons (2026-02-12)

**Experiment logging**: Every lab experiment should have notes in commit message or README — "what we tested, what we found, what decision was made." `ψ/lab/` files without context become archaeology puzzles in 3 months.

**Session checkpoints for research marathons**: For 7+ hour research sessions covering multiple topics, use /forward at every major topic transition to preserve context. Single continuous session across RAG → LightRAG → Thai Legal → Pipeline risks context drift and lost nuances.

**Morning session deserves /rrr too**: Short sessions (permissions, schedule setup) also have decisions worth capturing. Don't skip /rrr just because the session was "small."

**Architecture decisions to document**: When choosing OCR engine (ADE vs Gemini Flash), document the comparison: cost, quality, rate limits, resumability. Evidence-based decisions are only valuable if the evidence is preserved.

**Handoff quality matters**: A well-written handoff (`2026-02-12_14-06_thai-legal-rag.md`) means the next session can start immediately without re-reading 438 minutes of history.
