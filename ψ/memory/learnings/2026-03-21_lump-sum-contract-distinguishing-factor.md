# Lump Sum Contract: ผลสำเร็จของงาน vs BOQ Quantity

**Date**: 2026-03-21
**Context**: Deep reading of อ.2050/2559 and อ.148/2554 — both สัญญาเหมารวม cases with opposite outcomes

## Pattern

Two Supreme Administrative Court judgments on สัญญาเหมารวม (Lump Sum) reach opposite conclusions:

### อ.2050/2559 — Court ALLOWED deduction
- Contractor did 96 sqm of flooring instead of 260 sqm specified in BOQ
- Court: BOQ is part of the contract (ข้อ 2.5 ผนวก 5) — contractor must complete per BOQ
- Even though it's "เหมารวม", the contractor can't claim full pay when they didn't do all the specified work
- **But**: can only deduct proportional material+labor costs (82,000), NOT profit/overhead (12,300) — excess deduction must be returned

### อ.148/2554 — Court DENIED deduction
- Contractor drilled 56m instead of 150m for a well, but got the required 5 ลบ.ม./ชม. water
- Court: "สัญญาดังกล่าวกำหนดหลักการไว้กว้างๆ" — the contract defined success broadly (get water OR drill to 150m)
- Contractor achieved the functional objective (ผลสำเร็จของงาน) — got the water
- Employer cannot unilaterally reduce payment when the work objective is met

## Distinguishing Factor

The key difference is **whether the contract's functional specification was met**:
- **Met** (148): Got the required water → full pay, no deduction allowed
- **Not met** (2050): Didn't cover the specified area → proportional deduction allowed

It's NOT about whether it's called "เหมารวม" — it's about whether the contractor delivered what the contract *functionally* required.

## For RAG

This nuance is critical for answering questions about สัญญาเหมารวม — the system should surface BOTH cases and explain the distinguishing factor, not just cite one.
