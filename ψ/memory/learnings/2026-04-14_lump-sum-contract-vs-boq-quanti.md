## Lump Sum Contract: ผลสำเร็จของงาน vs BOQ Quantity

Two Supreme Administrative Court judgments on สัญญาเหมารวม reach opposite conclusions:

**อ.2050/2559 — Court ALLOWED deduction**: Contractor did 96 sqm instead of 260 sqm in BOQ. BOQ is part of the contract → contractor must complete per BOQ. Can deduct proportional material+labor costs, NOT profit/overhead.

**อ.148/2554 — Court DENIED deduction**: Contractor drilled 56m instead of 150m but achieved required 5 ลบ.ม./ชม. water. Contract defined success broadly → contractor achieved the functional objective → employer cannot unilaterally reduce payment.

**Distinguishing factor**: Whether the contract's FUNCTIONAL specification was met — NOT whether it's called "เหมารวม".
- Met (148): Got the required water → full pay
- Not met (2050): Didn't cover specified area → proportional deduction
